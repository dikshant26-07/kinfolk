What We Offer

Four Services. One Reliable Partner.

01

Website Building

Your digital presence, done right from day one.

We design and build fast, professional websites tailored to your startup's brand, audience, and goals. From landing pages to full multi-page sites, we handle everything — design, development, content structure, and launch.

What's Included

  • Custom design aligned to your brand
  • Mobile-first, fast-loading build
  • SEO-ready structure from the start
  • Ongoing support post-launch
02

HR Support

Build your team the right way, from the start.

Hiring your first few people is one of the most critical — and risky — things a founder does. We help you set up the people infrastructure that scales: job descriptions, offer letters, onboarding flows, and HR policies that protect both you and your team.

What's Included

  • Job description and hiring frameworks
  • Offer letters and employment contracts
  • Onboarding process design
  • HR policy documentation
03

Legal Support

Get the legal groundwork right before it becomes a problem.

Legal mistakes early on are expensive to fix later. We help startups get incorporated correctly, draft the right agreements, and stay compliant — without the cost of a full-time legal team.

What's Included

  • Company registration and incorporation
  • Founder and co-founder agreements
  • Client and vendor contracts
  • Compliance and regulatory guidance
04

Marketing Help

Get in front of the right people, with the right message.

Early-stage marketing is about clarity and consistency, not big budgets. We help you define your brand voice, build a content strategy, and run campaigns that actually reach your target audience.

What's Included

  • Brand positioning and messaging
  • Content strategy and creation
  • Social media setup and management
  • Campaign planning and execution
